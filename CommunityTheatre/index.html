About Our Theatre

We believe theatre is for everyone! Our community theatre brings together talented performers, dedicated volunteers, and passionate audiences. From unforgettable musicals to exciting dramatic productions, we create experiences that bring people together. Whether you are on stage, behind the scenes, or sitting in the audience, you are part of our theatre family.
